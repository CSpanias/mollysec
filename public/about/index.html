About Me

map[name:mollysec]

Who Am I?#

I’m a penetration tester who gets frustrated by “just because” answers. I want to understand not just how things work, but why; and then figure out how to make them better.

Currently, I work at a consultancy handling everything from CIS benchmarks to Active Directory assessments (my main focus), along with web and API work. Before this, I spent a couple of years in Fintech doing internal pentesting.

I’m working toward a Diploma (who knows, maybe a BSc!) in Cybersecurity, and I’ve picked up a few industry certifications along the way, including the OSCP, CRTP, and CAPE.

Why mollysec?#

I’ve been keeping my notes organized at x7331.gitbook.io for a while now; it works well as a tidy notepad for walkthroughs and quick references. But I found myself wanting to dig deeper into certain topics, and Gitbook just wasn’t the right format for that kind of writing.

So I created this site as a space to explore subjects I’m genuinely interested in and want to understand at a deeper level. Expect deep dives into Active Directory mechanics, workflow optimization, and the technical “why” behind common security practices.

Fair warning: I’ve only been in this field since early 2024, so there will likely be inaccuracies in my posts. I’m documenting my research as I learn. That said, I’m always open to discussion and critical feedback if you spot something worth correcting or exploring further.

Welcome to MollySec. I hope you find something useful here.

Oh, and MollySec? That’s named after my dog, Molly. As everyone knows, every good research project needs a mascot!
